this page describe what you if you choose a association as your class when making your character.
this choice matters and will more or less define your character in combat. you will gain more skills than the ones listed here over time as you level up. if you want to pick one of these just ask me about the skills i don't feel like writing all of them

ZWEI: you get a Zwei hander as your starting weapon and start with the passive guardian which gives you shield when you take dmg for a teammate and "your shield"  which lets you mark a teammate so you take dmg for them

TRES: you get the ability so create shoddy replicas of weapons you encounter as you level up your copies get better and the passive weapons know-how which increases all weapons dmg

SHI: start with a odachi gain bonus sneak attack damage and has the skill "flying sword" which gives haste and deals dmg and the passive eye of death which increases dmg based on dead enemies

CINQ:  start with a rapier and the skill duel declaration which applies "dueling" to the opponents  forcing them to target them   and the passive one step ahead which give you poise based on haste

LIU: Start with your choice of either gauntlets, a long sword, or unarmed. has the skill all-out war which applies burn in a aoe
and the passive intense blaze do bonus dmg based on burn

SEVEN: start with a rapier or long sword, has the skill fleche which does dmg and lowers enemy resist to a dmg type of your choice and the passive grasping vulnerabilities which lets you see enemy weaknesses

DEVYAT: start with a courier trunk either blunt or sharp,  start with the skill "a moment please" which halves your courier trunk stacks only works 3 times unless in a boss encounter
and the passive speedy delivery which gives haste based on courier trunk stack

DIECI: start with either unarmed or a key, has the skill fixated study which burns knowledge and does bonus dmg based on amount used and the passive diligent learning which gives you dmg up based on insight or skills used/ forgotten

OUFI: start with a halberd and the skill final warning applies contract to a opponent does dmg   and the passive verify fulfillment which does bonus dmg to those with contract and even more to those who break it
