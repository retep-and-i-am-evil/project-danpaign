For every 10 stacks of tremor, Target's AC is lowered by one. Upon inflicting Tremor Burst, the target with Tremor must make a Constitution saving throw with DC being equal to the Tremor stack. On fail, the target is Stunned. After Tremor Burst, Tremor on a target is halved. A creature may only have a maximum of 99 Tremor. Tremor is removed after 2 consecutive rounds of not being hit.
Tremor Burst is stated on skills.
