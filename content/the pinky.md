the pinky the most mysterious finger with not much being known about them. Members of the Pinky hide amongst the rest of society, infiltrating other Syndicates and organizations, including even the other Fingers. Members keep their relationship with the Pinky a secret, and because they only tend to encounter each other under specific circumstances, are almost always unaware of another's status as a Pinky member, even when directly interacting with each other. Members typically operate independently unless they have been given orders to do otherwise, and may even spend their 'free time' in conflict with other members of the Pinky.
there is no hierarchy within the pinky as all of the members are equal(not in strength though)
