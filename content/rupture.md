A creature that has the Rupture status is unaffected by it until they are critically hit. The creature that critically hit them can then choose to burst the rupture, causing the ruptured creature to take gluttony thunder damage equal to their amount of rupture stacks, with the damage increasing by 1.1x at 45 stacks and above. After a rupture burst, a creature loses all current rupture stacks. A creature loses half of their rupture stacks after having not taken damage for a full round. A creature may only have a maximum of 99 Rupture
