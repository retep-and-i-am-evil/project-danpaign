#### Protection

Gain a +1 to AC and lower damage taken by 1 for every stack. Lose stack at turn end.

#### Haste

Increase initiative by 1 and movement speed by 10ft per stack of Haste. If movement speed doubles your base movement due to Haste stacks, you may ignore difficult terrain and may disengage as a free action. Haste is reduced to 0 at the end of your next turn. Only one stack of haste may benefit from Untraceable Speed's speed increase.

#### Offense Level Up

Increase damage rolls by one dice tier per stack. Lose stack at turn end.

#### Defense Level Up

Decrease damage dice tier of attacks taken per stack. Lose stack at turn end.

#### Aggro

While you have Aggro stacks, all hostile creatures must make a Wisdom saving throw when within 10ft of you at the start of their turn. The DC for this save is equal to 10 + Number of Aggro stacks on self. On failed save, creatures must target you with their next hostile action. Hostile actions are defined by any attempt to harm or debilitate a creature. When a creature fails this save, they are immune to it for the next minute. Aggro stacks decrease by 1 at the start of the user's turn.
