You can staunch your bleed with an action, reducing your bleed by 8 and not taking damage. A downed creature that is bleeding will lose a death save at the start of their turn. When a creature receives healing, it loses Bleed stacks equal to half of the healing. For Bloodfiends, whenever a creature dies, any bleed they have or would receive turns into Bloodfeast.
